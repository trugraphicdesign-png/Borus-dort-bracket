Felix Nmecha Scores the “Top Goal of the Month” for September with a Bicycle Kick

On September 12, 2026, Nmecha leaped with his back to the Paderborn goal, volleyed Marcel Sabitzer’s cross, and finished with an acrobatic strike to seal the 3-0 victory. A picture-perfect bicycle kick—his first ever at the professional level. “I think that was the most beautiful goal of my career. It felt really good. The moment I struck the ball, I knew: It’s going in!” he said afterward.
